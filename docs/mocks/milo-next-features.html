Rep ranges and automatic progression

In the plan you write a range instead of a fixed number: 3×8–12. While you're inside the range Milo pushes reps; the day every set reaches the top, it moves the weight up for next time and the range starts again at the bottom. Fixed plans like 3×10 keep working exactly as today.

Plan. Type 3×8–12. The green tag shows the rule; tap it to pick the step (1, 2.5 or 5 kg).
Session. The range sits on each set; "beat it" says what moves you up.
Last set logged: 12 · 12 · 12. Milo tells you right away, together with the new 12-rep record.
Next Thursday. The load is already 72.5 kg and the goal goes back to 8+ reps.

Estimated 1RM and records per rep count

"70×12" and "75×8" become one comparable number: what you could lift once (Epley formula, sets of 1 to 12 reps). Progress now shows that number, so a day with fewer reps but more weight still counts as progress. Records are kept per rep count: heaviest for 3, 5, 8, 10, 12 and 15 reps.

Progress › Lifts. Est. 1RM by default; "Best load" shows what you have today.
Tap a lift. The est. 1RM line and a grid of rep records.

Protein, calories and off-plan meals

You enter a food's label once (per 100 g, or per piece) and every meal that uses it shows its calories and protein. Today gets a "Food today" card against your targets. When you eat something else, you tick the meal with what you actually ate, so the day stays honest and the plan stays the same.

Today (whole screen). The Food today card, macros under each meal and a swapped lunch. The weigh-in isn't due, so it shows the countdown.
Tap a meal. "As planned" ticks it like today; "Something else" asks what, with your foods one tap away.
Plan › Meals. Each meal totals itself; tap a food to enter its label once.
Add a food. Your foods, then Milo's built-in list of ~300 basics, then packaged products from Open Food Facts.
Scan the barcode. Packaged food comes in with its label already filled in, per 100 g or per serving.
Built-in list (works offline)About 300 basic foods (chicken, rice, oats, eggs, fish, fruit, olive oil…) with calories, protein, carbs and fat per 100 g, names in English and Portuguese. From USDA FoodData Central, which is public domain, so it ships inside Milo.
Packaged food: Open Food FactsThe open, crowd-sourced product database (strong in Portugal and Spain). Search by name or scan the barcode; your server asks Open Food Facts and the result is saved as one of your foods, so it's only fetched once.
Your own foodsAnything can be typed from the label or edited. A food you add belongs to you; changing it updates every meal that uses it.

Weigh-in every N days · Push reminders

You choose how often you weigh in (every day, 7, 15 or 30 days). On the other days Today shows "next in 4 days" in a quiet outline; on the due day it gets highlighted and a reminder arrives at 08:00. Reminders use the same web push as Holo: turn them on once per phone, then choose which ones you want.

Settings (whole screen). Weigh-in frequency, food targets, reminders and Apple Health in one place.
Reminders on the lock screen. Each one says what's next and can be answered without opening Milo.
Weigh-in day. The timeline highlights it first thing in the morning.

Apple Watch and Apple Health

A web app can't read Apple Health directly, so an iOS Shortcut does it on your iPhone: when a workout ends on the Apple Watch, it sends Milo the time, active calories and heart rate; every morning it sends your latest weight. Milo matches the workout to the day's session (and a walk or run to the planned cardio). The Shortcut uses a personal key you can revoke any time.

Set-up, once. Copy the key, add the Shortcut, turn on two automations.
Session summary. The Watch numbers arrive a few seconds after you end the workout on the Watch.
What comes inWorkout duration, active calories, average and max heart rate, the heart-rate curve, distance for walks/runs, and body weight from a smart scale.
What doesn't changeSets, reps and loads are still logged in Milo: the Watch doesn't know them. Calories burned don't touch the day score.
CardioAn Indoor Walk on the Watch fills in the treadmill minutes on its own. If you already logged them, Milo keeps yours.

Decisions taken in this mock (tell me if any should change)
  1. Protein and calories don't count for the day score, like the weigh-in: they're shown, not scored. (Alternative: replace "meals ticked" with "protein reached".)
  2. Progression step defaults to 2.5 kg and is set per exercise. It only applies to plans written as a range.
  3. Estimated 1RM uses sets of up to 12 reps; sets of 13+ still count for rep records but not for the 1RM.
  4. Reminders default: meals at their time, workout 18:00, weigh-in 08:00 on the due day, streak-at-risk 21:00. All can be switched off.
  5. Apple Health goes through a Shortcut + personal key (no App Store app needed). Android/Google Fit is not included.
