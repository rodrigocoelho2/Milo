BodyOps · new interface

Mock for review before anything is built. Your plan, meals, weigh-ins and last Tuesday's loads are real data, translated to English. Today's logged sets, Monday's session and the PR are examples.

What changes

  • Today is the home screen. At the gym you open the app and log, without scrolling past charts.
  • Sets replace the "real" text field. Tap a set to log its reps; load has − / + steppers. Volume and PRs come from the sets, so nothing is guessed from "3x12" text.
  • Last session sits on every exercise (load, reps and date), so you know what to beat.
  • Diet and Training merge into Plan, with one day picker, copy-to-days and the muscle map beside the editor.
  • Week replaces the dashboard. One ribbon shows meals and training for all 7 days; charts, muscle map and consistency sit underneath.
  • Accounts replace the shared token. Each person signs up with a username and password and gets a plan of their own. The first account is the admin and can reset passwords.
  • Settings holds your account, backup import/export and reset week, which used to crowd the sidebar.
